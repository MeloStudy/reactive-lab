# Recommended Datasets for Reactive Labs

This document contains a curated list of open-source datasets suitable for practicing Reactive Programming patterns (transformation, filtering, aggregation, and backpressure).

## 1. Technology & Software
- **GitHub Archive**: Full record of public events on GitHub (Commits, PRs, Stars).
  - **Format**: JSON (Multi-line).
  - **Use Case**: Real-time event processing and trending analysis.
  - **Link**: [GHArchive.org](https://www.gharchive.org/)

## 2. Gaming
- **Steam Games Dataset**: Metadata for thousands of games on Steam.
  - **Format**: CSV.
  - **Use Case**: Categorical filtering and price analysis.
  - **Link**: [Kaggle - Steam Store Games](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nikdavis/steam-store-games)

## 3. E-commerce & Retail
- **Electronic Products Prices**: Price listings across multiple stores.
  - **Format**: CSV.
  - **Use Case**: Price comparison and currency normalization.
  - **Link**: [Kaggle - Electronic Products Prices](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/datafiniti/electronic-products-prices)
- **Amazon Customer Reviews**: Millions of reviews across various product categories.
  - **Format**: Parquet/TSV.
  - **Link**: [Amazon Reviews Dataset](https://registry.opendata.aws/amazon-reviews/)

## 4. Transport & Urban
- **NYC Taxi Trip Data**: Gold standard for high-volume CSV processing.
  - **Format**: CSV/Parquet.
  - **Use Case**: Geospatial analysis and performance testing.
  - **Link**: [NYC TLC Data](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page)
- **Flight Delays & Cancellations**: US domestic flight records.
  - **Format**: CSV.
  - **Link**: [Kaggle - Flight Delays](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/usdot/flight-delays)

## 5. Entertainment
- **Netflix Titles**: Catalog of movies and TV shows.
  - **Format**: CSV.
  - **Link**: [Kaggle - Netflix Shows](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows)

## 6. Sports
- **International Football Results**: Historical match results since 1872.
  - **Format**: CSV.
  - **Link**: [Kaggle - International Football](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/martj42/international-football-results-from-1872-to-2017)

## 7. Food & Social
- **Food Recipes & Interactions**: Recipes and user ratings.
  - **Format**: CSV.
  - **Link**: [Kaggle - Food.com Recipes](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shuyangli94/food-com-recipes-and-user-interactions)
